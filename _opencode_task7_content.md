## Yoga Combinations vignettes (use the Gajakesari replacement from the task

**Sasa Yoga (शश — "the hare"), one of the five Panchamahapurusha Yogas (पञ्च-महापुरुष = "five great-persons")**

Sasa belongs to this set of five, formed when Shani — Saturn, our teacher of patience and structure — sits in a kendra (केन्द्र, a pivot-point: houses 1, 4, 7, or 10) from the Lagna (लग्न — the ascendant, or rising point), while occupying its own sign, or Swakshetra (स्वक्षेत्र — "own field"): Capricorn or Aquarius, or its exaltation sign, or Uccha (उच्च — "high place"): Libra. Classically this is read as discipline that becomes authority: staying power long enough that institutions begin to answer to you. Barack Obama's chart is among the better-documented celebrity examples I've come across — his birth data comes from an official record, and multiple Jyotish writers place his Saturn in Capricorn, in the first house, a kendra, which they read as satisfying the yoga. Even a well-attested placement like this one describes temperament and timing, not a promise; what a person does with Saturnine staying-power remains theirs to choose.

**Neechabhanga Raja Yoga (नीच-भङ्ग — "cancellation of debility")**

Neecha means low or debilitated; bhanga means breaking. Classical texts describe several distinct ways a debilitated planet's weakness can be undone — case by case, rather than by one fixed formula. The most commonly cited route: the planet that rules the debilitated planet's own sign sits strong in a kendra, usually counted from the Lagna (लग्न, the ascendant) though some readings count from the Moon instead. When the cancellation holds, the very placement that looked like a weakness converts into a raja yoga (राज-योग — "king-combination"), a combination read for standing and reach. It is one of my favorite teaching cases for how this system refuses to let any one placement be a final verdict. In Barack Obama's chart, retrograde — Vakri (वक्री) — Jupiter sits debilitated in the first house, but Saturn, the planet classical writers use to cancel that particular debility, sits right there with it in the same kendra; some who work with his chart connect the pattern to the Jupiter Mahadasha (महादशा — "great period," one of the long planetary cycles the tradition uses to time a life) that began for him in the mid-1990s, though I'd treat any such life-event tie as illustrative rather than demonstrated. I offer this one with real humility about method, since astrologers reason through "cancellation" case by case rather than by fixed formula — but as a teaching case for how difficulty transforms with the right supporting strength, it earns its place in any curriculum. As with every yoga here, this describes a pattern of potential, not a determined outcome for the person, and not a verdict on your own chart.

**Hamsa Yoga (हंस — "the swan"), one of the five Panchamahapurusha Yogas (पञ्च-महापुरुष = "five great-persons")**

Hamsa is Jupiter's turn among these five great-person combinations: Guru in a kendra from the Lagna (लग्न, the ascendant, or rising point), seated in its own sign (Swakshetra) — Sagittarius or Pisces — or its exaltation sign (Uccha): Cancer. The swan in our tradition is said to separate milk from water, so this yoga reads as discernment: a teacher's moral clarity rather than mere cleverness — a description of temperament, not a verdict on any person's character. Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, aerospace scientist and later President of India, is the example most often reached for: several charts drawn up for him place Jupiter in Sagittarius, its own sign, sitting in a kendra from the ascendant. His birth date is well recorded, though published birth times vary enough across sources that I'd treat the exact ascendant and house as the commonly reported version rather than a settled one; study the pattern, and hold the specific chart lightly.

**Budhaditya Yoga (बुध-आदित्य — "Mercury-Sun")**

Budha is Mercury, our planet of language and analysis; Aditya is the Sun, the seat of authority and visibility. When the two conjoin in the same house, tradition reads it as a mind built for articulate, public-facing work — administration, commentary, performance — so long as Mercury isn't burned too close to the Sun's glare, a state classical writers call Asta (अस्त — "set"): combustion, when a planet sits so close to the Sun that its own light is said to be overwhelmed, which can complicate the reading. Amitabh Bachchan is commonly cited alongside Dr. Manmohan Singh and Dr. Kalam, with Sun and Mercury reported together in a stellium — a cluster of several planets in one house — in the eighth house alongside Venus and Mars; I want to flag plainly that Bachchan himself has said he was born in the small hours of the morning, while most astrology sites in circulation use an unconfirmed afternoon time — a discrepancy noted openly even by the databank sites that track it. Take this one as illustrative of the yoga's meaning rather than a verified chart — a good reminder that the map is never quite the territory until the birth record is certain, and that what any of this describes is a capacity, not a fixed judgment of the people named here.

**Chandra-Mangal Yoga (चन्द्र-मंगल — "Moon-Mars")**

This is a wealth-combination of a different temperament than the standard house-lord Dhana Yogas (धन-योग — dhana = "wealth"): Chandra, the Moon, and Mangal, Mars, conjoined in the same house are read as generating resources through boldness and appetite for risk rather than quiet accumulation; some texts extend the yoga to a mutual-kendra relationship between the two as well, though that reading is looser and not every source accepts it. Bill Gates is the example most often named alongside it. Sources I've compared don't agree with one another on the exact geometry — some describe Mars and Moon opposite each other across the seventh house, others place Mars in the fourth — so while his birth date is public, I would treat any specific house claim here as instructive rather than settled. What the yoga teaches stands regardless of the exact degree: a chart can build wealth through calculated daring, and recognizing that temperament in your own placements is worth more than pinning down any one celebrity's exact hour of birth.

5 · Bird From a Name (Pañcha Pakshi)
Identify someone else's Pañcha Pakshi bird from the first vowel sound of their name, for when their birth star is unknown (Table No.4) — a separate method from the reference person's own birth-star bird above, and independent of everything else on this page.
On precision. Sun & Moon use truncated Meeus-type series (≈1–5′ accuracy). Mercury–Saturn use two-body Keplerian elements (perturbations ignored, ≈0.2–1° drift possible). Rāhu is the mean node. Good enough to teach the method; not a substitute for a certified ephemeris at a house-cusp boundary or eclipse-grade work.

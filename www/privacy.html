PL0 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

PL0 is a Jyotish (Vedic astrology) almanac and chart app. It works on your device and has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no advertising or analytics.

What the app stores

Birth details you enter (name, date, time, place), chart choices and settings are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

What leaves your device

Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else. The app makes no network requests: its typefaces, calculations, storage and optional alert scheduling are all on your device. The app does not use your device location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Notifications and alarms

If you turn alerts on, the app schedules notifications on your device for times such as tithi and nakshatra changes, planetary hours and Pañcha Pakshi peaks. They are calculated and delivered locally. On Android these use the notification permission and the exact-alarm permission so they arrive on time; the app also asks to restart them after the phone reboots.

Children

PL0 is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

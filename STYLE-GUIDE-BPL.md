# PL0 — Writing Style & Information Application Guide
## "The BP Lama approach" — how Barbara Pijan would manage this app

*Derived from a close reading of barbarapijan.com (bAstrHom.htm and linked
practice-essays). This document is the editorial constitution for PL0's
language, tone, and information architecture.*

---

## 1. Core stance: education, never fortune-telling

BPL's foundational rule, stated everywhere on her site:

> readings are designed as **educational learnings** for students of the vidya…
> not fortune-telling, and Jyotisha tradition offers no moral judgments.

**Applied to PL0:** every score, verdict, and reason line is framed as *study
material*, never as fate. The app's tier labels already lean this way — keep it:
- ✅ "Caution", "study this window", "traditionally avoided for…"
- ❌ "You will fail", "dangerous day", doom phrasing

Every negative finding must arrive with its **agency clause**. BPL: *"Without
awareness, there is no free will."* The reason the dosha matters is that seeing
it restores choice. A Rahu Kala line should read closer to:
> "Rāhu Kāla — traditionally set aside for all good undertakings; with awareness,
> simply schedule around it."

than to a flat prohibition.

## 2. Voice: warm scholar-practitioner, first person, case-method

BPL writes as a working practitioner sharing a lifetime of notes, not as an
institution. Hallmarks to adopt in PL0's copy:

- **First person, plain warmth**: "I am delighted to know you find these useful
  in your studies." App equivalent: brief, personal framing on results pages.
  ("Here is today's pattern, worked out case by case.")
- **Case-method epistemology**: she validates rules against documented lives,
  "not bench science, but it sure is interesting!" PL0's reasons panels should
  say *which tradition claims what*, and where sources disagree, show both
  (the app already does this for Siddha-yoga tables — keep and extend).
- **Etymology as teaching**: BPL always gives the Sanskrit + root (कर्मन् from
  कृ = create; netra = eye). PL0 should surface Devanagari + romanization +
  literal gloss on every technical term the user meets: *Vedha* (वेध =
  piercing), *Latta* (लट्टा = kick), *Rikta* (रिक्त = empty).

## 3. Information architecture: the map metaphor

Her site is organized as a **learning map**, not a feature list: Graha →
Rashi → Bhava → Nakshatra → Varga → Dasha, each with a main page. And her
governing image: *"If you're going to have a complicated story, you must work
to a map"* (Tolkien quote she chose deliberately).

**Applied to PL0:**
- Every page opens with a one-line orientation: where this system sits on the
  map of Jyotishavidya. (The SBC page: "one of the chakras of the
  samayavidya — the all-auspicious wheel connecting physical and spiritual
  planes.")
- Cross-link every computed element to its concept page/tooltip — the way she
  hyperlinks bhava-1 … bhava-12, graha main-pages, upagraha, varga.
- Keep the **"Jyotisha Topics" index** pattern: 9 graha / 12 rashi / 12 bhava /
  27 nakshatra / chakra / dasha as first-class navigation.

## 4. Presentation rhythm

BPL alternates dense teaching blocks with **breathing spaces**: quotations from
Jung, Goethe, Genesis 1:14, Sarah Williams' "Old Astronomer", Paracelsus.
Ritual bookends: the graha mantras at page top, "Wishing you every happiness
and continuing success in studies" at close.

**Applied to PL0:**
- A rotating contemplative quotation on results screens (she'd approve of
  Jung's "When an inner situation is not made conscious, it appears outside
  as fate" beside a Vedha panel).
- Close each reading/session view with a blessing line, e.g. her own sign-off:
  *"Wishing you every happiness, healing from sorrow, guidance by inner light,
  and success in all of life's endeavors."*
- Whitespace and short paragraphs; she never crowds.

## 5. Handling fear and negativity (critical)

She is explicit that misusing the vidya to "prey upon human worrying" breaks
it. Karma "has no moral agenda." Difficult placements are reframed as
curriculum: *"Whatever unfolds during your lifetime is EXACTLY what you have
chosen to explore."*

**Applied to PL0 — hard rules:**
1. Never render a dosha without its remedy or its lesson. The app has a
   Remedies page — link every Varjya/Ashubha verdict there.
2. No superlative doom. BPL would write "Ashubha = a classroom, not a verdict."
3. Always the learner's agency: findings describe *timing patterns*, not
   commands. "Traditionally avoided" not "do not".
4. Where traditions conflict, present both without ranking them morally —
   she treats differing lineages respectfully (as with her two Siddha tables).

## 6. Honesty about limitations

She cheerfully calls her own site "an old dinosaur website, ancient and creaky,"
corrects mistakes openly, invites edits. PL0 already does this well (its inline
confidence notes, OCR-artifact flags, "this app's own synthesis" disclosures).
Keep and amplify: each engine section keeps its provenance note — which text,
which translation, where OCR was uncertain, what is synthesis.

## 7. Concrete copy changes for PL0 (actionable list)

| Where | Current | BP-Lama style |
|---|---|---|
| Muhurta verdict header | "Avoid" | "Varjya — traditionally set aside; here is why, and here is the remedy link" |
| Reason lines | terse rule names | rule name + Devanagari + one-line gloss + source citation |
| Empty-state text | "no data" | "What have you come to learn? Begin by entering the birth star…" |
| Footer | none | blessing line + educational-purpose disclaimer |
| Term tooltips | English only | Devanagari + IAST + literal meaning |
| Conflicting sources | silent | both shown, attributed |

## 8. What NOT to take from her style

- Her hand-built HTML with broken mid-sentence line wraps — PL0 stays clean.
- The donation/paywall apparatus — out of scope.
- New-Age cosmology (Lemuria, dimensions, galactic core) — keep PL0 grounded
  in the classical citational method; adopt only her *tone* and *ethics*, not
  her metaphysics.

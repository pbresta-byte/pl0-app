## Jyotish Books Scout — feature suggestions (2026-08-24)

*Generated by the automated Feature Scout workflow. These are grounded, non-duplicate proposals meant to be appended into this file. They deliberately avoid everything already in sections A/B above and everything already shipped (SBC, panchāṅga + dosha checklist, classical time windows, Parāśara-hora activity layers, Pañcha Pakshi, calendar, Antara Chakra, Aṣṭakavarga, Vimśottari, Aṣṭakūṭa, Muhūrta Chintāmaṇi checks, horary, commodities, remedies, Saptarṣi/Bṛhaspati cycles, moment-gravity Kāla-bala/Vimśopaka office engine). Ordered roughly by value/impact.*

---

1. **Seasonal & annual muhūrta prohibition gate (Malamāsa · Kharmās · Chāturmāsya · Guru/Śukra Asta · Ayana)**
   The single biggest gap for a real electional tool: the "big-calendar" prohibitions that override every fine-grained tithi/nakṣatra score. No auspicious saṁskāra (marriage, gṛha-praveśa, upanayana) is undertaken during an Adhika-māsa (intercalary lunar month), during Kharmās (Sun transiting Dhanu/Sagittarius = Dhanur-māsa, and Mīna/Pisces = Mīna-māsa), during Chāturmāsya (Devaśayanī to Devotthānī Ekādaśī, Viṣṇu asleep), or while Jupiter or Venus is combust (Guru/Śukra Asta) — combust benefics invalidate a marriage muhūrta outright. Uttarāyaṇa is preferred over Dakṣiṇāyana. Surface these as a top-of-page banner that can veto or heavily flag any elected window.
   - *Classical basis:* Muhūrta Chintāmaṇi; Dharmasindhu / Nirṇayasindhu (saṁskāra-kāla rules); standard combustion (Asta) orbs for Jupiter/Venus.
   - *Implementation:* Adhika-māsa is detectable client-side (a lunar month containing no solar saṅkrānti — the app already computes new-moon instants and can compute Sun's sign-ingress times). Kharmās/Ayana are direct reads of the Sun's sidereal longitude (already available). Guru/Śukra Asta needs Sun–Jupiter and Sun–Venus angular separation against fixed combustion orbs (positions already computed). All lookups, no new ephemeris.

2. **Muhūrta Lagna Śuddhi — quality of the ascendant at the elected instant**
   The app scores the *panchāṅga* of the moment thoroughly but never grades the rising sign, which classical muhūrta treats as decisive. Implement the standard lagna-śuddhi checklist for the elected moment's chart: benefics in kendra/trikoṇa strengthen; malefics belong in 3/6/11 only; the 8th house and lagna should be free of malefics; the Moon should avoid the 6th/8th/12th; and Pāpa-kartari (malefic scissors around the lagna) is a defect. This closes the loop with the existing neutralization idea (B-11) by giving it the lagna it needs.
   - *Classical basis:* Muhūrta Chintāmaṇi (lagna-prakaraṇa); Bṛhat Saṁhitā / BPHS house-strength conventions; the "benefics in quadrants dispel evil" clause already cited as R28.
   - *Implementation:* The engine already computes the ascendant (per the MCP `panchanga` tool) and all graha sign positions. Compute whole-sign house of each graha from the lagna, apply the benefic/malefic-by-house checklist, and emit tone-tagged reason lines. Reuses the existing functional benefic/malefic classifier.

3. **Panchaka Rahita muhūrta (Mṛtyu · Agni · Rāja · Chora · Roga panchaka)**
   A distinct, widely used electional filter separate from the nakṣatra-Panchaka the app already has. A numeric sum of the moment's tithi, vāra, nakṣatra and lagna (reduced mod 9) yields a remainder that flags one of five "panchaka" defects — Mṛtyu (death), Agni (fire), Rāja (royal wrath/penalty), Chora (theft) and Roga (disease) — each barring a specific class of act (e.g. Agni-panchaka bars anything with fire/kilns, Chora-panchaka bars travel with valuables). Clean remainders give a "panchaka-rahita" green light.
   - *Classical basis:* Muhūrta tradition (Panchaka Rahita tables, North-Indian pañcāṅga practice). Transcribe the exact remainder→panchaka assignment from one named printed source rather than hard-coding a remembered mapping, per the app's own provenance discipline.
   - *Implementation:* Pure arithmetic on values the app already has (tithi #, weekday #, nakṣatra #, lagna sign #). A single lookup table for the five named defects plus their per-defect activity caution. Cheap, high-visibility.

4. **Varṣaphala (Tājika annual chart) tab — Muntha, Varṣeśa, Sahams, Ithaśāla/Iśarāpha, Mudda daśā**
   The app has a "Tājika-Varṣeśa-*style*" office test buried in the moment-gravity engine but no actual annual chart. A full Varṣaphala tab for the user's birth data would compute the solar-return chart, the Muntha (progressed point advancing one sign per year of age), the Varṣeśa (year-lord elected among the five office-bearers by pañcavargīya bala), the Sahams (Puṇya, Vidyā, Yaśas, Kārya, Roga sensitive points), the Tājika aspects Ithaśāla (applying, within deeptāṁśa orb — a forming yoga) and Iśarāpha (separating), and the Mudda/Patyāyinī daśā for the year.
   - *Classical basis:* Tājika Nīlakaṇṭhī; Praśna Tantra. The Sahams and Ithaśāla/Iśarāpha orb machinery are standard Tājika.
   - *Implementation:* Needs a solar-return solver (find the instant the transiting Sun equals natal Sun longitude — a 1-D root-find on the existing ephemeris) plus deeptāṁśa orb tables per graha. Muntha and Sahams are simple longitude arithmetic. Non-trivial but self-contained and client-side.

5. **Ghaṭa Chakra — personalized "killer-factor" avoidance by janma-rāśi**
   A personalized inverse of the activity layer: for the user's Moon sign, each rāśi has an assigned ghātaka (destroyer) tithi, vāra, nakṣatra, yoga, karaṇa, prahara (day-segment), lagna and lunar month that are individually inauspicious for that native to begin work. Surfaced as a compact "your danger factors today" card and folded into the daily grade as a personalized penalty layer.
   - *Classical basis:* Muhūrta texts' Ghaṭa/Ghātaka Chakra tables (Muhūrta Chintāmaṇi; Kālaprakāśikā tradition).
   - *Implementation:* A static 12-row table (rāśi → its ghātaka factors) checked against the day's already-computed tithi/vāra/nakṣatra/yoga/karaṇa/lagna and the user's stored janma-rāśi. Pure lookup; pairs naturally with the existing Chandrāṣṭama personalization.

6. **Kemadruma & Chandra-ādhi yoga check for the muhūrta Moon (Sunāphā · Anaphā · Durudhurā)**
   Classical muhūrta rates a Moon with no supporting planets as a serious defect: Kemadruma (no graha in the 2nd or 12th from the Moon, none conjunct) weakens any election, while Sunāphā (planets in the 2nd), Anaphā (12th) and Durudhurā (both) progressively strengthen it. The app grades the Moon's nakṣatra and tārā but never its yoga-context in the sky.
   - *Classical basis:* Bṛhat Jātaka / BPHS candra-yoga chapter; carried into muhūrta as a Moon-strength qualifier.
   - *Implementation:* From the already-computed graha longitudes, test which planets occupy the signs 2nd/12th from the Moon (excluding Sun by convention), classify into Kemadruma/Sunāphā/Anaphā/Durudhurā, and emit a strength line. Trivial given existing positions.

7. **Grahaṇa & node-proximity avoidance (eclipse and Gaṇḍānta-of-the-luminaries alert)**
   Muhūrta practice bars auspicious undertakings around eclipses and for a window on either side. Detect when the Sun or Moon is within eclipse orb of Rāhu/Ketu (a solar or lunar eclipse) and flag a several-day exclusion band, plus a lighter caution when the Moon is closely conjunct a node (nodal Gaṇḍānta). This complements the tithi-Gaṇḍānta idea (B-10) with the luminary-node dimension.
   - *Classical basis:* Standard grahaṇa-doṣa in all muhūrta literature (Bṛhat Saṁhitā eclipse chapters; Kālaprakāśikā); avoid saṁskāras during and immediately around eclipses.
   - *Implementation:* Sun/Moon vs Rāhu/Ketu angular separation against latitude-aware eclipse orbs — all longitudes already computed. Even a purely longitude-based "eclipse-likely" flag (Sun/Moon within ~15° of a node at syzygy) is a useful client-side approximation, clearly labeled as such.

8. **Sixteen-saṁskāra muhūrta profiles (per-rite nakṣatra/tithi/vāra tables)**
   The current activity layer has ten generic profiles; classical muhūrta specifies distinct auspicious nakṣatra/tithi/vāra/karaṇa sets for each of the traditional saṁskāras — Nāmakaraṇa, Anna-prāśana, Cūḍākaraṇa, Karṇavedha, Vidyārambha, Upanayana, Vivāha, Gṛha-praveśa, Bhūmi-pūjā, etc. Adding these as first-class rite profiles makes the elector materially more precise than the coarse "ceremony" bucket.
   - *Classical basis:* Muhūrta Chintāmaṇi and B.V. Raman's *Muhurtha* (per-saṁskāra prakaraṇas).
   - *Implementation:* Extend the existing `gradeActivity` profile table with new rite keys and their allowed nakṣatra/tithi/vāra sets. Same machinery, more rows; each row carries its source citation per the app's convention.

9. **Jaimini layer — Chara Kārakas, Kārakāṁśa lagna, and Chara Daśā**
   A natal-page complement that the BPL synthesis practically asks for: compute the seven/eight Chara Kārakas (Ātmakāraka etc. by descending degree), the Kārakāṁśa (the navāṁśa sign of the Ātmakāraka projected onto the rāśi chart), and the sign-based Chara Daśā. Kārakāṁśa is the technical cousin of the svāṁśa (navāṁśa-nakṣatra of the lagna) that STYLE-GUIDE-BPL flags as the headline identity marker, so it slots directly into the existing natal reading.
   - *Classical basis:* Jaimini Sūtras; S. Rath's Jaimini works (already in the verification corpus family).
   - *Implementation:* Kārakas are a degree-sort of the seven grahas (needs exact longitudes — available). Kārakāṁśa reuses the D9 engine already present. Chara Daśā is a sign-progression with standard duration rules; well-specified and deterministic.

10. **Yoginī Daśā — the eight-fold Moon daśā as a Vimśottari cross-check**
    An independent daśā system (36-year cycle over eight Yoginīs — Maṅgalā, Piṅgalā, Dhānyā, Bhrāmarī, Bhadrikā, Ulkā, Siddhā, Saṅkaṭā, of 1–8 years) started from the Moon's nakṣatra. Practitioners run it alongside Vimśottari to confirm timing; showing both side by side fits the app's "where traditions agree/disagree, show both" ethic.
    - *Classical basis:* Yoginī Daśā tradition (Deva Keralam / Nārada lineage); standard 8-Yoginī assignment from janma-nakṣatra.
    - *Implementation:* Start-Yoginī = (nakṣatra number + 3) mod 8; the app already has the Moon's nakṣatra and fractional position for the balance-of-daśā, so this reuses the exact scaffolding built for Vimśottari.

11. **Vāra–Tithi yoga grid (Siddha · Amṛta · Maraṇa · Hutāśana · Dagdha · Viṣa)**
    A cheap panchāṅga table the app has only partially (R14 class-based Siddha). The full weekday×tithi grid yields named daily yogas — Siddha and Amṛta (grade up), and Maraṇa, Hutāśana ("fire-consumed"), Dagdha ("burnt") and Viṣa (grade down) — plus the standalone Dagdha-tithi list per weekday. High information density for near-zero compute.
    - *Classical basis:* Muhūrta tradition's Dina-tithi yoga tables (Muhūrta Chintāmaṇi; Kālaprakāśikā). Keep separate named tables where sources differ, as the app already does for the two Siddha traditions.
    - *Implementation:* One 7×15 lookup keyed by (weekday, tithi-in-pakṣa) → yoga name + tone, evaluated against values already in hand. Slots straight into `classical-rules.js` beside R13–R17.

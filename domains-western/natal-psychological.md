## The Sun-Moon-Ascendant Triad (Alan Leo's foundation)
Western psychological astrology begins with three primary factors:
1. **Sun** = the essential self, vitality, purpose, the father principle
2. **Moon** = the emotional nature, habits, the mother principle, receptivity  
3. **Ascendant** = the personality, physical body, how one appears to others

Cross-reference to Jyotish: Sun ≈ Atmakaraka (soul significator), Moon ≈ Manahkaraka (mind), Ascendant ≈ Lagna/Tanu Bhava.

## Aspects (Western) vs Drishti (Jyotish)
| Western | Degrees | Nature | Jyotish equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conjunction | 0° | Unifying | Sambandha/Yuti |
| Sextile | 60° | Opportunity | Shadashtaka (not classical) |
| Square | 90° | Challenge | Kendra relationship |
| Trine | 120° | Harmony | Trikona relationship |
| Opposition | 180° | Polarity | Samasaptaka |

Jyotish uses whole-sign aspects (all planets aspect certain houses based on their position); Western uses degree-based orbs.

## The Four Elements & Three Modes
Elements: Fire (spiritual/creative), Earth (material/practical), Air (mental/social), Water (emotional/intuitive)
Modes: Cardinal (initiating), Fixed (sustaining), Mutable (adapting)

These combine into 12 signs, each a unique element+mode pairing.
Cross-reference: Jyotish tattvas (Agni/Prithvi/Vayu/Jala) map directly to Fire/Earth/Air/Water.

## Evans' "Twelve Doors to the Soul"
Evans frames each house as a door through which the soul enters experience:
Door 1 = Identity, Door 2 = Values, ... Door 12 = Transcendence.
This parallels BPHS's bhava significations but with psychological rather than material emphasis.

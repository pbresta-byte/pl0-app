# Western Spiritual & Esoteric Astrology — Dee, Agrippa, Weor

- John Dee, *The Hieroglyphic Monad* (1564) — 52,358 chars

## Hermetic Principles
Dee's Monad unifies all planetary symbolism into a single glyph — the spiritual
equivalent of the Jyotish bindu (बिन्दु point-source). All manifestation proceeds
from unity to multiplicity through planetary stages.

## Agrippa's Occult Correspondences
Agrippa maps every level of reality to planetary correspondences:
- Planets → Metals (Sun=Gold, Moon=Silver, Mercury=Quicksilver, Venus=Copper, Mars=Iron, Jupiter=Tin, Saturn=Lead)
- Planets → Stones (Ruby/Sun, Pearl/Moon, Emerald/Mercury, etc.)
- Planets → Angels (Michael/Sun, Gabriel/Moon, Raphael/Mercury...)

This parallels Jyotish planetary associations: Sun→ruby→gold→Agni, Moon→pearl→silver→Apas.

## Weor's Esoteric Zodiac
Weor interprets each sign as a stage of initiatic development:
Aries = beginning of the Great Work... Pisces = completion/dissolution.
This maps to Jyotish moksha-oriented signs (12th house/Pisces as liberation).

## Cross-tradition notes
- Agrippa's correspondences ≈ Jyotish ratna (gemstone), dhatu (metal), and devata associations
- Weor's zodiac-as-initiation ≈ Jyotish atmakaraka journey through signs toward moksha
- Dee's Monad ≈ Jyotish Om (ॐ) as the primordial sound-symbol containing all grahas

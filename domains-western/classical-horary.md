## Essential Dignity (Western)
The Western system uses five levels of dignity:
1. **Domicile** — planet in its own sign
2. **Exaltation** — planet elevated
3. **Triplicity** — planet in element-compatible sign  
4. **Term/Bounds** — Egyptian terms
5. **Face/Decan** — 10° subdivisions

Cross-reference to Jyotish: Jyotish uses *uchha* (exaltation), *moolatrikona*, *swakshetra* (own sign), *mitrakshetra* (friend's sign). The Western term/bounds system has no direct Jyotish equivalent.

## Horary Method (Lilly/Bonatti)
1. Note the moment the question is asked (or letter received)
2. Cast the chart for that instant
3. Determine radicality: Ascendant degree not too early (<3°) or late (>27°)
4. Identify querent = Lord of Ascendant; Quesited = Lord of relevant house
5. Assess perfection: aspect between significators, translation/collection of light
6. Bonatti's 146 considerations provide the full checklist of judgment conditions

## Cross-tradition notes
- Western horary ≈ Jyotish prashna (both cast chart for moment of question)
- Western essential dignity ≈ Jyotish shadbala (six-fold strength)
- Western perfection via aspect ≈ Jyotish Ithasala yoga in Tajika
- Lilly's houses align with BPHS bhava meanings but with different emphasis

## Humoral Theory
Western medical astrology operates on the four humours:
- **Hot & Dry** = Choleric (Fire signs: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) — Mars/Sun
- **Hot & Wet** = Sanguine (Air signs: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) — Jupiter/Sun
- **Cold & Wet** = Phlegmatic (Water signs: Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) — Moon/Venus
- **Cold & Dry** = Melancholic (Earth signs: Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) — Saturn/Mercury

Cross-reference to Jyotish Ayurvedic doshas: Vata ≈ Air (Vata = movement), Pitta ≈ Fire (Pitta = transformation), Kapha ≈ Water/Earth (Kapha = structure).

## Planetary Rulership of Organs
- Sun → heart, vital force, right eye (male)
- Moon → stomach, fluids, left eye (female), mind
- Mars → muscles, blood, head, left ear
- Mercury → nervous system, lungs, speech, hands
- Jupiter → liver, fat, growth, right ear
- Venus → kidneys, reproductive system, throat, beauty
- Saturn → bones, teeth, joints, skin, longevity

## Cross-tradition notes
- Western zodiac-body mapping ≈ Jyotish Kalapurusha body parts per sign/house
- Western humors ≈ Jyotish tridosha (Vata/Pitta/Kapha)
- Culpeper's herbal-planet correspondences ≈ Jyotish planetary herbs in Vedic Remedies
- Both traditions use the 6th house for disease and the 8th for chronic/death

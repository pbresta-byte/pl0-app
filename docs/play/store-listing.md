**Short description (80):** Daily Vedic almanac: panchanga, Pañcha Pakshi, muhurta, dashas and yogas.

**Full description (4000):**
PL0 is a personal Jyotish almanac that runs entirely on your phone.

Each day it shows the panchanga (tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, vara), planetary hours, Rahu Kalam and a muhurta gauge that scores the day's windows. Pañcha Pakshi shows the ruling bird and its activity through the day, with alerts for the best and lowest times.

Enter birth details to see your chart with divisional charts, Vimshottari and other dashas, shadbala, and the yogas your chart forms. Each yoga that has a written result explains what it gives and when it forms.

Choose your own conventions in Options: ayanamsha, scoring, and where competing classical readings differ, which reading to follow. Optional alerts tell you when the tithi or nakshatra changes, at planetary hours, ingresses, stations and Pañcha Pakshi perfect times.

Private by design: no account, no ads, no analytics. Your birth data stays on your device.

English and Spanish.

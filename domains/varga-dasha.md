# PART I — DIVISIONAL CHARTS (VARGA)

## 1. Purpose & Philosophy of the Vargas

- **Saravali** (quoted by Goel): "The effect of a horoscope should be predicted according to the strength in divisions of houses without which one cannot take even one step in astrology." (Goel, l.191–193)
- Thousands of births occur in any two-hour Lagna span with near-identical Rashi charts; divisional charts answer how fates differ. (Goel, l.196–198)
- Goel: "The birth chart is the source of light and direction. The divisional charts are the many diffracted rays carrying its own definite waves." (Goel, l.199–201)
- Raj Kumar: two-fold aim of a varga — (a) study one specific house/aspect of life, (b) find the true strength of planets connected with that house; each zodiac division has a corresponding time division, so a varga shows possibilities in different time frames, viewed through the Dasha sequence. (Raj Kumar, About the Book, l.10–20)
- Rath's Varga Chakra: every cycle of twelve divisions is a **harmonic** depicting levels of consciousness, gross and subtle. (Varga Chakra, l.1000–1006)

## 2. The Five Harmonic Cycles (S. Rath / Sarbani Sarkar, Varga Chakra)

| Cycle | Range | Plane | Key charts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1st (physical) | D-1…D-12 | Physical plane; persons in 3-D experience | D-1 body; D-2 wealth; D-3 siblings; D-4 property/fortune; D-7 progeny; D-9 spouse; D-10 career; D-12 parents |
| 2nd (first harmonic) | D-13…D-24 | Conscious plane | D-16 luxuries/vehicles/happiness; D-20 spiritual life; D-24 higher learning |
| 3rd (second harmonic) | D-25…D-36 | Sub-conscious — inherent strengths/weaknesses | D-27 strengths/weaknesses; D-30 evils |
| 4th (third harmonic) | D-37…D-48 | Super-conscious; ancestral legacy | D-40 matrilineal; D-45 patrilineal legacy |
| 5th (fourth harmonic) | D-49…D-60 | Supra-conscious; past-birth karma | D-60 |

Of cycle 1, "the Rashi (D-1), Drekkana (D-3) and Navamsa (D-9) are considered to be the most important." (Varga Chakra, l.1012–1014)

## 3. Varga Schemes & Divisional Dignities

### 3.1 Schemes
- **Shadvarga** (prashna): Rasi, Hora, Drekkana, Navamsa, Dwadasamsa, Trimsamsa.
- **Saptavarga** (mundane): adds Saptamsa.
- **Dasavarga** (universal horoscopy; Phaladeepika/Mantreshwara): adds Dasamsa, Shodasamsa, Shastyamsa.
- **Shodasavarga** (Parasara's 16; originally royal horoscopy): all above plus Chaturthamsa, Vimsamsa, Chaturvimsamsa, Nakshatramsa, Khavedamsa, Akshavedamsa.
- Complete set of twenty adds Panchamsa (D-5), Shashthamsa (D-6), Ashtamsa (D-8), Ekadasamsa (D-11). Ashtakavarga is a dot/dash numeric scheme for planetary/sign strength. (Varga Chakra l.1066–1090, 1490–1520)
- Minimum for proper delineation: six vargas. (Goel, l.347–350)

### 3.2 Vimshopaka Bala weights (Goel, l.352–450)
- Shadvarga: D1=6, D2=2, D3=4, D9=5, D12=2, D30=1.
- Saptavarga: D1=5, D2=2, D3=3, D7=2.5, D9=4.5, D12=2, D30=1.
- Dasavarga: D1=3, others 1.5 except **D60=5 — Shashtiamsha carries more weightage than the birth chart**.
- Shodashvarga: D1=3.5, D9=3, D16=2, D30=1, D60=4, rest 0.5–1.

### 3.3 Vaiseshikamsas / benefic-placement ladder
Count of charts within a scheme where a planet occupies exaltation, moolatrikona or own sign:
- Shadvarga: 2 Kimsuka, 3 Vyanjana, 4 Chamara, 5 Chatra, 6 Kundala.
- Saptavarga: counts 2–6 same as shadvarga; 7 = Mukuta (crowned king).
- Dasavarga: 2 Parijata, 3 Uttama, 4 Gopura, 5 Simhasana, 6 Paravata, 7 Devaloka, 8 Brahmaloka, 9 Airavata, 10 Sreedhama (Lakshmi's abode).
- Shodasavarga: 2 Bhedaka, 3 Kusuma, 4 Nagapushpa, 5 Kanduka, 6 Kerala, 7 Kalpavriksha, 8 Chandanavana, 9 Poornachandra, 10 Uchchaisrava, 11 Dhanvantari, 12 Sooryakanta, 13 Vidruma, then Indrasana, Gangaloka, Sri Vallabha (16).
(P.V.R. Narsimha Rao chapter, Varga Chakra l.1490–1660; identical ladder in Goel l.560–650; Goel notes Phaladeepika uses dasavarga counting exaltation/own/friendly signs.)

Illustration (Goel l.650–720): Dr. Rajendra Prasad — Jupiter benefic in 8 of 10 dasavarga positions = Surlok/Brahmlok, "gives the status of Maharaja."

### 3.4 Varga Vimshopaka formula (Goel, l.504–560)
Planet weight in one chart = (chart Vimshopaka Bala × Varga Vishwa Bala)/20; Varga Vishwa Bala by panchadha maitri placement: own/exalt 20, ati-mitra 18, mitra 15, neutral 10, enemy 7, bitter enemy 5. Exalted/own = 100% of chart bala; bitter enemy = 25% floor. Goel argues panchdha maitri should be recomputed per divisional chart (temporary friendship changes there).

## 4. Construction Methods
- Parasara's method: sequential counting, regular or irregular; regular order = "order of Brahma," synchronous with creation, applying to matters of the living being — navamsa and saptamsa are typical Brahma divisions. Mantreshwara-type methods divide by multiples of twelve or the division number. (Varga Chakra l.1090–1105)
- Longitudes inside divisional charts (Goel l.660–750): multiply natal longitude-without-sign by division number N; drop completed signs (Sun 13°04′ × 7 = 91°28′ → 1°28′). Alternative: subtract end of preceding division, multiply remainder by N. Unequal Parashari Trimsamsha: (A−B)×30/C.

## 5. General Rules for Reading ANY Divisional Chart (Goel, l.956–1050)
1. Well-placed key trio: D1 Lagna lord + that varga Lagna lord + varga karaka; best trines/angles, next 2nd/11th; 3,6,8,12 bad even if exalted.
2. Exceptions: Mars in 6th good anywhere; Mercury in 8th favourable anywhere; Mercury in 8th of D4, Saturn in 8th of D8, Venus in 12th of D9 good.
3. Strong varga-Lagna required for effects to manifest (strong D7 Lagna assures progeny); aspects or shubha-kartari strengthen it.
4. Raj yoga gives status to the varga's signification (D12 raj yoga = high-status parents); dhana yoga multiplies it (D9 dhana yoga = wealthy/multiple spouses); peak during their dasha periods.
5. Planet badly placed in D1 AND in 8th/12th in a varga fails; if it is the varga Lagna lord, auspicious results fail entirely.
6. Vargottama applies to ALL vargas: highly auspicious provided Lagna lord well placed — else "gives dreams only."
7. Combustion / planetary-war defeat spoils a planet even well placed; worst in varga 3/6/8/12.
8. Debilitated planet helps only if debilitation cancelled.
9. Power order: exaltation > vargottama > own sign > digbala.
10. Karaka in varga Lagna behaves like karaka-in-bhava — BAD: Mercury in D24 Lagna spoils education; Jupiter in D7 Lagna hits progeny; Venus in D9 Lagna bad for spouse; Sun/Moon in D12 Lagna hit father/mother. Multiplied by malefics, relieved by benefics.
11. Planets in 3rd/8th of a varga are "death-like" for its signification (spouse in D9, profession in D10). Sun in 8th of D8 kills father.
12. Rahu/Ketu/varga-8th-lord in varga Lagna on a mooltrikona sign → chronic stress in that varga.
13. Cruel divisions rising in shadvarga Lagna → short life, poverty, wickedness.
14. Timing: select sub-periods of planets strong w.r.t. the varga Lagna; transits apply in vargas.
15. BPHS: good divisions fall in signs owned by lords of angles from their own arudha; combust/war-defeated/debilitated/weak-awastha lords destroy combinations. A varga whose Lagna rises in a combust planet sign fails to deliver its good significations.

## 6. Individual Vargas — highlights

- **D2 Hora**: magnified 2nd house — wealth, family, speech, eating habits. Parashari (Uma Shambhu) hora uses only Cancer/Leo: odd signs first half Sun/Leo second Moon/Cancer; even signs reversed — wealth through parental blessing (Sun=father, Moon=mother). Other systems catalogued by Goel: Labh Mandook/Kerala hora, Poorva Parashari, Venkatesha, Sun-Moon hora, Vikram Mandook, Cyclical. Rath workshop treats Hora as time rulership (hour), space rulership, guna, fruits of karma, half-day symbolism.
- **D3 Drekkana**: co-borns, courage, ability; 22nd drekkana (khara); death timing/nature; disease identification via presiding deities/rishis; body parts; thieves' nature; mundane use. Goel covers Garga/Varanasi/Somnath/Jagannath drekkanas, sexual inclinations, sex of co-born, genetic link.
- **D4 Chaturthamsha**: destiny, immovable property, home, settlement abroad.
- **D7 Saptamsha**: children/grandchildren; sex of children; Beeja sphuta & Kshetra sphuta.
- **D9 Navamsha**: spouse, marriage, dharma, supplementary chart; Atmakaraka in navamsa, Karakamsa & desires, Ista Devata (moksha) from Karakamsa (Varga Chakra ch.12). Exception: Venus in 12th of D9 good.
- **D10 Dasamsa**: career, honour, status, promotion/demotion.
- **D12 Dwadasamsa (Suryamsa)**: parents, grandparents, lineage, pre-natal; karakas for relatives; arudha & argala.
- **D16/D20/D24/D27/D30**: movable assets/happiness; upasana & fruits of worship (conscious plane — "Aham brahmasmi"); learning; inherent strengths/weaknesses; miseries/evils.
- **D40/D45/D60**: matrilineal legacy; patrilineal legacy; all effects benefic/malefic — heaviest weightage in dasavarga/shodashvarga schemes.
- Non-Parashari: D5 spiritual inclination/poorva punya; D6 disease/debt/disputes; D8 longevity/accident; D11 unearned income/speculation.
- Higher: D72 (navamsa-ashtamsha), D108 (dwadasamsa-navamsha), D144 (dwadasamsa-dwadasamsa), D150 Nadiamsha, D300 ardh-Nadiamsha; Ashta-navamsha, Nav-navamsha; "sex of horoscope."
- Linking of vargas (Goel ch., TOC l.360–380): interlinking charts of one individual; family members; husband-wife; property division; business relations; guru-disciple.

## 7. Raj Kumar's synthesis (Vargas: A Vedic Approach)
Combines PAC/yoga analysis with the Nadi system (sign/nakshatra/sub lords of a planet) to obtain "true strength"; comparative study of the same lord/karaka across Rashi and varga charts; dasha sequence as the viewing light of each varga.

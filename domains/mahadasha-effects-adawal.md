# Classical Mahādaśā Effects — Shanker Adawal, *Encyclopedia of Vedic Astrology: Dasa Systems*

Source: `C:/Users/drrag/ansel/1050 jyotish books with siderial zodiac/Jyotish_Encyclopedia of vedic astro_dasa systems_Shanker Adawal.pdf`, extracted via `pdftotext -layout` to a working `.txt` (18,553 lines) on 2026-09-16. All quotes below are transcribed verbatim from that extraction (OCR ligature artifacts like "bss"→"loss", "ofthe"→"of the" left as-is in the txt-line citation but silently corrected in the `en` strings actually wired into the app, since those feed a live UI, not a scholarly transcript).

This is the "Vimsottari Dasa System of Predictions" chapter (ch. 2). Each of the 9 grahas gets: (1) a general-effects opening paragraph independent of dignity, (2) a strong/well-placed branch, (3) a weak/badly-placed branch. Used for a new "Classical Mahādaśā effects" panel on the Dasha page, gated by this app's own existing dignity-tier engine (`dignityTierInSign`) to pick the strong/weak/general branch — own/exalted/friend → strong branch; enemy/debilitated → weak branch; neutral → general paragraph only. This is a genuinely different, complementary layer to the app's pre-existing `dashaLordReading`/`dashaFunnelNote` mechanism (which derives its reading from THIS app's own Lagna-based functional house-lordship engine, not from a classical dasha-effects text) — both are kept, not merged.

## Sun (txt-line 7101-7168)
> "During the progress of the Dasa of the Sun, the general effects to be noted are; accumulation of wealth through wickedness, Kings and battles. There may be trouble from fire, beasts; eyesore, dental and abdominal troubles, serious condition of wife or children, separation from elders and preceptor, loss of servants and property." (7105-7109)

Strong (7114-7119): "If the Sun be strong benefic and well placed in the horoscope... he will bring about birth of children, give power and position, fame, favours from elders, superiors and people in power and authority. There will be success in undertakings, and prosperity to father."

Weak (7121-7127): "If the Sun be weak, malefic and badly placed... he will cause ill health, diseases like bilious excitement, burning sensation of the body, epilepsy, heart diseases, eye trouble, skin diseases... loss of wealth, Bad reputation, criminal prosecution, imprisonment, danger to father and quarrels in the family."

## Moon (txt-line 7703-7728)
> "During the Dasa of the Moon, the native may get fame and wealth through Vedic hymns, Deities, Brahmins and state favour. He may also secure wealth and lands through women. He may get in plenty flowers, clothes, ornaments, perfumes and other objects of enjoyment of varied taste. He may be opposed by the wicked, suffer loss of fortune and suffer from rheumatism or gout." (7710-7715)

Strong (7720-7723): "If the Moon be strong, benefic and well placed... there will be tranquillity of mind, success in business, enjoyment of good food and comforts, birth of children, marriage, gains from lands and houses."

Weak (7725-7732): "If the Moon is weak, malefic and badly placed, she will cause diseases like sleeping disease, drowsiness, diseases of the lungs... There will be ill health or danger to mother, wife and children, trouble from female servants, mental worries, loss of ancestral property."

## Mars (txt-line 8177-8203)
> "During the Mahadasa of Mars there will be attempts of money making through lands, litigation and the royal favour, acquisition of more cattle and lands; quarrel, and disputes with brothers and sons; life with wicked women and suffer from diseases caused by disturbances of bile, blood and through fire." (8181-8185)

Strong (8190-8193): "If Mars be strong, benefic and well placed... there will be success in litigation, defeat of enemies, birth of brothers or prosperity to them, gains from landed estates, minerals... Fame will increase and the native will be raised to a position of command and power."

Weak (8195-8203): "If the planet Mars is weak, malefic and badly placed, there will be failure in litigations, quarrels in the family, ill health to brothers or their death, loss of property, danger from weapon wounds and poison... entanglement in criminal conspiracy."

## Mercury (txt-line 9416-9442)
> "At the time when Mercury Dasa is in progress the native enjoys the benefit of spiritual preceptors. He gets wealth through Brahmins, acquires new lands, cows and horses. He worships great men, gods and acquires great wealth. He is troubled by disease, caused by the disturbance of the three Humours- wind, heat and phlegm." (9423-9427)

Strong (9432-9435): "If Mercury be strong, benefic and well placed... there will be making of good friends, increased fame, respect from the learned, favours from elders, high position, gains from business, fluency of speech, learning."

Weak (9437-9442): "If Mercury is weak, malefic and badly placed; he will cause diseases like eye disease, throat troubles, tonsils... There will be displeasure from superiors, quarrels with friends, loss of wealth through wicked acts and business, forced journeys to distant places."

## Jupiter (txt-line 8600-8629)
> "At the time when Jupiter Dasa is in progress, the native gets new clothes and the like, gets attendants, retinue and similar grandeur. He enjoys great respect and dignity. He is blessed with children, wealth and friends. He is adored for eloquence in his speech. The native is likely to be separated from his elders in family, and suffers from ear troubles and phlegmatic problems." (8607-8612)

Strong (8617-8622): "If Jupiter is strong, benefic and well placed... there will be increase of wealth and fame, respect from elders and preceptors, birth of children, rising to high position, acquisition of vehicles, houses, and material comforts and success in undertakings."

Weak (8624-8629): "If Jupiter is weak, malefic and badly placed, there will be Bad health or death of children, loss of wealth and position, scandals and intrigues with wicked people, and failure in undertaking."

## Venus (txt-line 10727-10754)
> "During the Dasa of Venus there is acquisition of wife, jewels, clothes, lustrous gems, wealth, ornaments, horses, comforts of the bed and sofas. There is profusion of wealth by trade, agriculture and voyages; anxiety, mental agony, separation or death of elders and misery to relatives." (10735-10739)

Strong (10744-10748): "If Venus be strong, benefic and well placed, there will be marriage, marital happiness, birth of beautiful daughters, acquisition of articles of luxury and comfort, vehicles and houses... respect and honours from people in high position and authority."

Weak (10750-10754): "If Venus be weak, malefic and badly placed, she will cause ill health or death of wife, intrigues with low woman and loss of wealth thereby, loss of splendour, low vitality... diseases like venereal complaints, anaemia, kidney and urinary diseases."

## Saturn (txt-line 9022-9051)
> "During the course of Saturn Dasa the native acquires wealth through war or other dislocations in the country. He gets attendant and old women; have cattle wealth such as cows, buffalos and bulls, distress to children and Wife; develops diseases due to wind and phlegm, piles, burning sensation in legs, hands and parts of his body." (9030-9034)

Strong (9040-9043): "If Saturn be strong, benefic and well placed... there will be gain of high position and authority, influence over masses and low class people, gain through servants, gain from lands and agriculture, cattle, business. There will be general prosperity and happiness."

Weak (9045-9051): "If Saturn is weak, malefic and badly placed, there will be loss of wealth, poverty, ill health, death of elderly members in the family, trouble through servants and low born people... misery, pains and rheumatic complaints."

## Rahu (txt-line 9830-9857)
> "During the Rahu Dasa the native takes to wicked means or suffers from the terrible ailment beyond diagnose; loses wife and children. There is danger of being poisoned and trouble from enemies and suffers from diseases of the eyes and head. The native faces opposition from friends and cultivators too and acquires displeasure of the kings." (9840-9844)

Strong (9849-9852): "If Rahu is strong, benefic and well placed, during his Major period there will be accumulation of wealth, gains from business, prosperity or promotion in profession, gain of position of command and respect, and general happiness."

Weak (9854-9857): "If Rahu is weak, malefic and badly placed, there will be loss of wealth, quarrels and disputes, criminal actions, wanderings, poverty, ill health to wife and children or their death, death of elderly members in the family."

**Source inconsistency flagged, not silently resolved** (txt-line 9859 vs 9905): the text states Rahu's exaltation sign is Scorpio in one place and Taurus a few paragraphs later — different classical schools genuinely disagree on this point (Taurus/Scorpio vs. Gemini/Sagittarius are both attested elsewhere in the literature); not adjudicated here, and this app's own existing dignity engine (whatever exaltation sign it already uses for Rahu elsewhere) is what actually gates the strong/weak branch, not this source's own contradiction.

## Ketu (txt-line 10440-10467)
> "During the Dasa of Ketu, the native will be oppressed by the enemies, thieves and the ruler; he will receive wounds from weapons, develop diseases caused by excessive heat, earn bad name, a stigma to his family; face danger from fire and find his living in a foreign land." (10445-10448)

Strong (10459-10461): "If Ketu is benefic, strong and well placed, during his Major period there will be gain of wealth, gains through evil actions, well being and general prosperity." (note: "gains through evil actions" is the source's own phrasing, not a transcription error — kept verbatim.)

Weak (10463-10467): "If Ketu is weak, malefic and badly placed, there will be loss of wealth, ill health, imprisonment, association with low class people, fear from thieves and diseases of chronic and acute nature."

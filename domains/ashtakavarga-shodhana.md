# Ashtakavarga Śodhana — Trikoṇa & Ekādhipatya Reduction Rules

**Status: TRANSCRIBED-VERIFIED**
**Source:** C. S. Patel, *Ashtakavarga System of Prediction* (1957 ed.), Chapter III: "Trikona & Ekadhipatya Shodhanas," Slokas 1–9. This is an English translation of the Ashtakavarga chapter of the *Brihat Parāśara Hora Śāstra* (BPHS) with transliterated Sanskrit verses and commentary.

---

## 1. Scope: What Gets Reduced

The reductions are applied to each **Bhinnāṣṭakavarga** (the individual 12-sign Ashtakavarga of each planet — Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn). After both reductions are complete on each Bhinna, the **Sarvāṣṣṭakavarga** (Samudayāṣṭakavarga / "Rāśi Ashtakavarga") is constructed by summing the post-reduction bindu counts across all seven Bhinnas sign-by-sign. The reductions are NOT applied a second time to the Sarva aggregate itself.

From Patel's Chapter V, note on Sarvachanchachakra (p. 66):
> "This Chakra can be prepared from the Sayana positions also and the effects of the transits of the planets can be studied therefrom. For other purposes like **Trikona and Ekadhipatya reductions**, etc., the Nirayana positions should be considered."

From Chapter III, Slokas 1–3 and Notes, the reductions are explicitly described as operating on "a planet's Ashtakavarga" (i.e., each Bhinna individually), with examples given for the Sun's, Moon's, Mars', Mercury's, etc. Ashtakavargas separately.

## 2. Part I: Trikona Śodhana (Trine Reduction) — First Pass

**Source:** Patel 1957, Chapter III, Slokas 1–3, pp. 37–40.

### 2.1 The Four Triads

> "Slokas 1-3 -- Trikonas (trines) are as follows :--
>
> 1. Mesha, Simha and Dhanus are trine to one another ;
> 2. Vrishabha, Kanya and Makara ,, ,, ,, ,,
> 3. Mithuna, Tula and Kumbha ,, ,, ,, ,,
> 4. Kataka, Vrischika and Meena ,, ,, ,, ,,"

### 2.2 Reduction Rules (Trikona)

> "In one set of a triad whichever house contains the least number of bindus, that number should be subtracted from all the three houses. When one of the three houses is void of bindus, no reduction is to be made in the other two houses. If the bindus are equal in all the three houses, remove all."

This gives four cases:

| Case | Condition | Action |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **1. All three unequal** | e.g., Mesha=4, Simha=3, Dhanus=2 | Subtract the minimum (2) from all three → 2, 1, 0 |
| **2. One house zero** | e.g., Mesha=4, Simha=3, Dhanus=0 | No reduction. Leave as-is: 4, 3, 0 |
| **3. Two houses zero** | e.g., Mesha=4, Simha=0, Dhanus=0 | Two schools: **(a) Parāśara**: no change; **(b) Mantreswara**: eliminate the third also → 0, 0, 0. Patel notes: "It is a rare case to have two houses without bindus in a planet's Ashtakavarga and much more so to have them in trine to one another. In this book the first method [Parāśara] is adopted." |
| **4. All three equal** | e.g., 3, 3, 3 | Remove all → 0, 0, 0 |

The book notes two schools of interpretation (p. 38–39):
- **First view (adopted by Patel)**: Subtract the single minimum value from all three.
- **Second view**: Reduce iteratively until all three are equal to the minimum.

> "One school accepts the view that the minimum number should be taken away from all the three houses; while the other school says that the reduction should be carried on till, all the three houses contain bindus equal to the minimum. In this book the first method is adopted."

**Note on Prasna Mārga** (p. 39):
> "The author of Prasnamarga says that in one set of a triad, if one/two houses contain no bindu, the bindus in the others/other house, should be eliminated."

### 2.3 Order of Application

Apply Trikona reduction to each of the four triads, for each planet's Bhinnashtakavarga independently, proceeding sign-by-sign through the chart.

## 3. Part II: Ekādhipatya Śodhana (Ownership Reduction) — Second Pass

**Source:** Patel 1957, Chapter III, Slokas 4–9, pp. 40–41.

### 3.1 When to Apply

> "Slokas 4-8 -- 1. After Trikonashodhana is over, Ekadhipatyashodhana is to be carried out."

The order is fixed: **Trikona first, then Ekāhipatya.** Never reversed.

### 3.2 The Ownership Pairs

Each of the five outer planets owns two signs:
- **Mars**: Mesha & Vrischika
- **Mercury**: Mithuna & Kanya
- **Jupiter**: Dhanus & Meena
- **Venus**: Vṛṣabha & Tula
- **Saturn**: Makara & Kumbha

The two luminaries own one sign each:
- **Sun**: Siṃha only → **no reduction** (single sign, no pair)
- **Moon**: Karkaṭa only → **no reduction** (single sign, no pair)

> "9. The bindus in Kataka and Simha should be retained as they are."

### 3.3 Reduction Rules (Ekādhipatya)

The commentary explicitly distinguishes **houses occupied by a planet** vs. **houses empty of planets** in the natal chart. For each planet-owned sign pair:

| Rule | Condition | Action |
|------|-----------|--------|
| **1. Single-sign ownership** | Kataka (Moon), Siṃha (Sun) | No reduction. Retain as-is. |
| **2. One house has zero bindus** (after Trikona) | e.g., Mesha=2, Vrischika=0 | No reduction. Leave both unchanged. |
| **3. Both houses empty of planets, unequal bindus** | e.g., Mesha=4 (empty), Vṛṣabha=2 (empty) | Subtract smaller from both → 2, 0 |
| **4. One house occupied (has planet), occupied house has FEWER bindus** | e.g., Mesha=2 (Mars there), Vṛṣabha=3 (empty) | Deduct smaller from unoccupied only → 2, 1 (occupied unchanged) |
| **5. One house occupied (has planet), occupied house has MORE bindus** | e.g., Mesha=3 (Mars there), Vṛṣabha=2 (empty) | Remove all bindus from unoccupied house → 3, 0 |
| **6. Both houses occupied by planets** | Mesha (Mars there), Vṛṣabha (Venus there) | No reduction. Leave both unchanged. |
| **7. Both houses empty, equal bindus** | Mesha=3 (empty), Vṛṣabha=3 (empty) | Remove all from both → 0, 0 |
| **8. Equal bindus, ONE house occupied** | Mesha=3 (Mars there), Vṛṣabha=3 (empty) | Remove bindus from unoccupied house → 3, 0 |

> "Notes -- Ekadhipatyashodhana means the reduction in respect of the two houses (rasis) owned by one common lord, e.g., Mesha and Vrischika of Mars, etc."

### 3.4 Concise Summary from the Sanskrit Verse (p. 44)

The book provides a compact restatement of the rules in its closing Notes on Sloka 9:

> "1. In Trikonashodhana subtract the minimum figure out of the three houses of a triad irrespective of the figures in them.
> 2. No reduction should be made when one house has no bindu.
> 3. If the three figures in the three houses are equal, remove all.
> In the Ekadhipatyashodhana the same rules apply with the exception that figures in the house occupied by a planet should not be changed."

## 4. Post-Reduction: Śodhyapinda (Longevity Dividend)

After both reductions, the remaining bindus in each planet's Bhinnashtakavarga are used to compute three "Pindas" (totals):

> "Sloka 9 -- After the two reductions are carried out, multiply the figures in Vrishabha and Simha by 10, those of Mithuna and Vrischika by 8, of Tula and Mesha by 7, of Kanya and Makara by 5 and those of the remaining rasis by their respective rasi numbers i.e., Kataka by 4, Dhanus by 9, etc. The sum of these products is known as Rasiguna Yoga or Rasipinda."

> "Again multiply the figures of the houses occupied by the Sun, the Moon, Mercury and Saturn by 5, that occupied by Mars by 8, Jupiter by 10 and Venus by 7. The sum of these products is called Grahaguna Yoga or Grahapinda."

> "The sum of Rasipinda and Grahapinda is known as Shodhyapinda, Shuddhapinda or Yogapinda."

## 5. Application to Sarvāṣṭakavarga (Rāśi Ashtakavarga)

**Important distinction**: The rules above apply to each **Bhinnashtakavarga** individually. The Sarvāṣṭakavarga (the 12-sign aggregate chart sometimes called "Rāśi Ashtakavarga") is constructed AFTER the reductions:

- For each of the 12 signs, sum the post-reduction bindu counts from all seven planetary Bhinnas (plus Lagna if included).
- The resulting 12 totals (ranging up to ~40 per sign, 337 aggregate) are the Sarva bindus.
- **The Trikona and Ekadhipatya reductions are NOT re-applied to the Sarva chart.** The Sarva is a derived aggregate of already-reduced Bhinna figures.

This is confirmed by Patel's worked example (Standard Horoscope, p. 42–43), where the Sun's Bhinnas goes through Trikona reduction (48 → 21) then Ekadhipatya reduction (21 → 18), and the Sarva is presented as the sum of the seven planets' post-reduction Bhinna figures (totaling 337).

**Variant practice note:** Some later schools (particularly in South Indian / KP traditions) are reported to apply the same two reduction passes directly to the Sarva chart itself, treating the Sarva sign-bindu counts as if they were a Bhinnashtakavarga. The astrologerraman.com source quotes the Ekadhipatya rules as applying to "Rasis" arrived at by Trikona Sodhana — suggesting this variant exists. However, the primary BPHS text as rendered by Patel applies the reductions only at the Bhinna level.

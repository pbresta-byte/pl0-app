## 1. Foundations of natal interpretation

**The classic ladder of judgment.** Phala Dipika (Mantreswara, G.S. Kapoor retyped) sets the method: first know what each *bhava* stands for (`Jyotish_Phala Dipika_G.S. Kapoor_retyped.txt : 596-611`), then overlay the planet's natural significations, then the lordship the planet carries in this chart. "If a house stands for a certain subject matter, the lord of the house will also be considered to be concerned with it… we have to consider also the natural or inherent characteristics" (: 602-606). So every prediction = **house signification × planet's natural karakatva × lordship × strength × dasa**.

**Brihat Jataka's structure shows the full scope of jataka**: signs & technical terms; planets; non-human births; conception (nisheka); peculiarities of birth; balarishta (infant mortality); ayurdaya (longevity — Varahamihira prefers Amsa/Pinda ayurdaya over dasas); ashtakavarga; profession; rajayogas; nabhasa yogas; bhadra yogas; planetary conjunctions; renunciation; nakshatra effects; Moon-in-sign; planets-in-sign; aspects; planets-in-houses; special combinations; misfortune; female horoscopy; death; lost horoscopes; decanates (`Jyotish_Brihat jataka P_S_Sastri_2007.txt : 57-89`). Note Varahamihira omits Rahu/Ketu entirely and does not use Vimshottari dasa (: 44-52) — a reminder that classical judgment predates the modern Parasari default stack.

**Lagna coloring rule.** A planet in the Ascendant stamps its temperament on the native: strong Mars in lagna = commander-like courage; weak Mars = petty quarrels. If the lagna is empty, the native takes on the appearance and character of the lagna lord; planets aspecting the lagna also inject their temperament (`Phala Dipika : 667-680`).

## 2. The twelve bhavas

House designations per Phala Dipika ch.1 (`: 540-580`):

| House | Key subjects |
|---|---|
| 1 (Tanu) | Body, self, present life (Deha, Udaya, Rupa, Seersha, Vartmana, Janma) |
| 2 | Wealth (Vitta), learning, food/drink, right eye, face, documents, speech, family (Kutumba) |
| 3 | Courage (Duschikya→parakrama), breast, right ear, army, prowess, younger brother |
| 4 | Home, land, mother, conveyance, happiness (Sukha/Hibuka), water, perfumes, ornaments |
| 5 | Progeny, intelligence, soul, knowledge of future, royal mark, belly, mantra/shastra learning |
| 6 | Debts, arms, thieves, wounds, diseases, enemies, paternal relations, battle, sin, humiliation |
| 7 | Desire, passion, spouse, road, people, sexual union |
| 8 | Mangalya (spouse's longevity for a wife), Randhra, longevity, sorrow, defeat, impurity, impediments |
| 9 | Guru, devata, father, poorva-punya (previous luck), worship, penance, virtue, grandson, noble family |
| 10 | Profession/trade, rank, honour, command, zenith, conduct, gait ("Meshurana" — issuing orders) |
| 11 | Gains, income, accomplishment, elder sibling, left ear, delightful news |
| 12 | Loss, misery, left eye, expenses, spies, last rites, poverty, sin, confinement |

Note the father-house debate: some hold father from the 10th (10th = 7th from 4th-mother); Mantreswara keeps father in the 9th because it is 5th-to-5th (`: 564-570`).

**House groupings** (`: 582-591`):
- Dusthanas (evil): 6, 8, 12; Leena (hidden): 3, 6, 8, 12
- Kendras (angular): 1, 4, 7, 10; Panaphara: 2, 5, 8, 11; Apoklima: 3, 6, 9, 12
- Upachaya (growing): 3, 6, 10, 11; Trikonas (trines): 5, 9 — "very auspicious"

**Bhava bala.** Sarvartha-style strength math appears across the corpus: a house is judged by the strength of its lord plus Dik Bala and Drig Bala of the house itself (`Phala Dipika : 1454-1489, 1888-1890`). Directional strength baseline: Sun/Mars strongest in the 10th (South), Moon/Venus in the 4th (North), Mercury/Jupiter in the 1st (East), Saturn in the 7th (West); nil at the opposite point (`: 1327-1339`).

## 3. Planets: karakas, bodies and temperaments

Natural significations (Phala Dipika ch.2, `: 612-666`):
- **Sun**: copper/gold, father, valour, victory, Atman, Shiva-worship, temples, yajna, enthusiasm.
- **Moon**: mother's welfare, tranquility of mind, sea-bathing, fruits/flowers, grains, fame, pearls, silver, beauty.
- **Mars**: strength, siblings, cruelty/battle/courage, weapons, thieves, kitchen fire, gold, wounds, commander-in-chief.
- **Mercury**: learning, eloquence, arts, praise by scholars, maternal uncle, wit, mechanical skill, friends, sister's children.
- **Jupiter**: knowledge, noble quality, progeny (Santan karaka), ministership, teaching, shastra-jnana, devotion to gods and brahmins, treasury, honour. In a female chart Jupiter is the husband-karaka; in a male chart the progeny-karaka (`: 636-644`).
- **Venus**: wealth, conveyances, clothes, ornaments, music-dance, scents, bed comforts, poetry, marriage. Wife/marital-happiness karaka in a male chart (`: 645-653`). Kapoor's worked example: marriage promised in Sun dasa (7th lord) actually came in Venus dasa because Venus is kalatra-karaka (`: 654-661`).
- **Saturn**: longevity, death, fear, downfall, disgrace, sickness, misery, poverty, debts, iron, service, confinement, stability, agricultural implements (`: 662-666`).

Physical/temperamental portraits used for appearance and disease-typing (`: 681-718`): Sun bilious, square-built, reddish-brown eyes; Moon wind-phlegmatic, fair, soft-spoken, rules blood; Mars youthful, curly hair, fierce look, rules marrow; Mercury green like durva grass, tridoshic, rules skin; Jupiter lion-voiced, huge body, phlegmatic, wealth-significator; Venus beautiful, broad-eyed, rules semen; Saturn lame-looking, deep-set eyes, windy, black-clad, old appearance. Disease nature follows the afflicting planet: Sun → bilious complaints, Saturn → windy disorders (`: 678-680`).

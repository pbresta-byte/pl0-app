## 1. Core Concept

**Muhurta / मुहूर्त** — from *muhur* ("moment, in a moment"); a unit of time (48 min) and by extension the science of electing auspicious moments. B.V. Raman: "Muhurtha could therefore be defined as that precious moment when the vibrations radiated by man are altered to a specific wavelength capable of entering resonance with the radiations of the same vibratory rate coming from other planets and stars" (*Jyotish_Muhurta_B.V. Raman.txt*:35). Time is personified as **Kalapurusha** (कालपुरुष), "Time-Person," the all-powerful container of creative/protective/destructive forces (*Raman*:25–28).

**Muhurta vs. horoscopy** — Raman's classic distinction: "Horoscopy is diagnostic... Muhurtha is prescriptive as well as preventive" (*Raman*:29). Horoscopy reads the karma one brings at birth; Muhurta shows how to "remove, neutralise, counteract or overcome the evils indicated by the horoscopic chart." Jung's dictum quoted approvingly: "whatever is born or done this moment of time has the qualities of this moment of time" (*Raman*:34).

## 2. The Panchanga — Five Limbs

The five limbs represent "the five sources of ethereal energy" which, properly secured, conduce health, wealth and prosperity (*Raman*:40).

| Limb | Sanskrit | Definition | Quality | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lunar day | Tithi तिथि | 12° differential longitude Sun→Moon | lunar energy = mental energy; "minute knowledge of tithis gives wealth" | *Raman*:41–47 |
| Weekday | Vara वार | day named for its predominant planet | planetary influence of that lord | *Raman*:59 |
| Constellation | Nakshatra नक्षत्र | Moon traversing 13°20' arc (800') | electromagnetic stellar radiation contacted by Moon | *Raman*:61–71 |
| Luni-solar period | Yoga योग | joint motion of Sun+Moon = 13°20'; formula (Sun+Moon)/13°20' per Surya Siddhanta | "strengthens our bodies, removes germs of disease" | *Raman*:72–93 |
| Half lunar day | Karana करण | multiple-of-6° separation; 11 karanas: Bava, Balava, Kaulava, Taitula, Garija, Vanija, Visti (movable, ×8 rotation) + Sakuna, Chatushpada, Naga, Kimstughna (fixed) | half-tithi quality | *Raman*:94–95 |

- Tithi ≈ 0.9483 day; lunar month ≈ 29.53 days (*Raman*:43). Bright/dark half determined by whether Moon−Sun < or > 180° (*Raman*:45).
- The three most important limbs for muhurta: tithi, nakshatra, vara (*Raman*:40).

## 3. Three Universal Checks for Any Election

(a) Tarabala, (b) Chandrabala, (c) Panchaka — "Otherwise an election chart will lose its significance" (*Raman*:107).

### 3.1 Tarabala (constellation strength)
Count from birth star (Janma Tara) to day star; mod 9 (*Raman*:108):
1 Janma — danger to body · 2 Sampat — wealth · 3 Vipat — danger/accident · 4 Kshema — prosperity · 5 Pratyak — obstacles · 6 Sadhana — ambitions realised · 7 Naidhana — danger · 8 Mitra — good · 9 Parama mitra — very favourable.

Cycle softening (*Raman*:110–112): first cycle (stars 1–9) evil holds fully; second cycle (10–18) evil only ~50%, concentrated in 1st pada of Vipat, 4th pada of Pratyak, 3rd pada of Naidhana; third cycle (19–27) negligible. Raman's practice note: still avoid Vipat and Naidhana for major works (journey, marriage, enterprise start) even in cycle 3 (*Raman*:113). Conflict noted below on stars 22 & 27.

### 3.2 Chandrabala (lunar strength)
Moon should not occupy 6th, 8th or 12th from Janma Rasi in the election chart (*Raman*:115). Example: Mrigasira native (Taurus rasi) marrying on Bharani day fails both tests — Bharani is Naidhana star and Aries is 12th from Taurus (*Raman*:116). Also: election chart must bear sympathetic connection to birth chart; a badly afflicted radical chart can offset any muhurta (*Raman*:38); if birth time unknown, use the Nama Tara (name-constellation) (*Raman*:38).

### 3.3 Panchaka
Five-source energy check of the day (detailed later in Raman ch. III/V) — used to screen days numerically.

## 4. Rejected Segments and Days (General Prohibitions)

- **Lagna Tyajya degrees** (*Raman*:96–97): avoid first 3° of Aries/Taurus/Sagittarius/Virgo (bhujanga, serpent force); last 3° of Pisces/Capricorn/Cancer/Scorpio (Rahu's force); middle half-ghati (13°30'–16°30') of Gemini/Libra/Leo/Aquarius (Gridhra, vulture force).
- Avoid **Tuesday and Saturday** for all auspicious work (*Raman*:98).
- Avoid **4th, 8th, 14th lunar days** both halves (Raman also flags 12th, but his experience: 12th is actually auspicious if other factors strong — *Raman*:99–100).
- **Tyajyakala per nakshatra**: each star has a negative period lasting 4 ghatis (1h36m), starting times listed in ghatis from sunrise — Aswini 50, Bharani 4, Krittika 30, Rohini 40, Mrigasira 14, Aridra 21, Punarvasu 30, Pushya 20, Aslesha 32, Makha 30, Pubba 20, Uttara 1, Hasta 21, Chitta 20, Swati 14, Visakha 14, Anuradha 10, Jyeshta 14, Moola 20, P.Shadha 20, U.Shadha 20, Sravana 10, Dhanishta 10, Satabhisha 18, P.Bhadra 16, U.Bhadra & Revati 30 (*Raman*:101–102).
- Strengthen ascendant + its lord + Moon in every chart (*Raman*:96).

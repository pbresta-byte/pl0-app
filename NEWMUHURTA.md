## A. Tithi classes (Nanda / Bhadra / Jaya / Rikta / Poorna)

**R1. Five tithi classes defined** — inputs: tithi number (1–15 per paksha).
> "31st, 6th and 11th lunar day are known as Nanda; 2nd, 7th and 12th … Bhadra; 3rd, 8th and 13th … Vijaya(Jaya); 4th, 9th and 14th … Rikta; 5th, 10th and 15th … Poorna."
— *Jyotish_Brihat Samhita_N.Chidambaram_ retyped_upto ch 59.txt* L11931-11935 (BS ch. LII); same in *Jyotish_Vedic remedies_S.Rath ban.txt* L3955-3957.

**R2. Rikta tithis (4/9/14) inauspicious for all works; other classes auspicious per Utpala** — input: tithi.
> "According to Utpala all the lunar days except the Rikta ones are auspicious."
— *Jyotish_Brihat Samhita_M.R.Bhat_part 2_1981.txt* L18502-18503 (comm. on BS XCV).

**R3. General electional tithi/vara quality set** — inputs: tithi, vara, paksha.
> "All odd tithis except the 9th are good. Of the even tithis the 2nd, 6th and 10th are auspicious. Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday are the best. Even Monday should be rejected when the Moon is waning. Saturday should be rejected as it connotes frequent thefts."
— *Jyotish_Muhurta_B.V. Raman.txt* L610.

**R4. Avoid-list tithis (Panchanga Suddhi)** — input: tithi.
> "In regard to lunar days, the 4th, 6th, 8th, 12th and 14th, full and new moon days should be avoided."
— *Jyotish_Muhurta_B.V. Raman.txt* L148.

## B. Karana qualities

**R5. Per-karana activity assignments (movable karanas)** — input: karana name.
> "In Bava Karana shall be done deeds of auspicious, moveable or fixed character… In Balava… acts of charity and help to Brahmins. In Kaulava… joyful deeds… In Taitila Karana… deeds liked by public at large, and houses shall be built. In Gara Karana lands shall be tilled, seeds sown, houses built… In Vanik Karana, works of a fixed nature… dealings with merchants."
— *Jyotish_Brihat Samhita_N.Chidambaram_ retyped_upto ch 59.txt* L11945-11958 (BS LII.6-7).

**R6. Vishti(Bhadra) karana = no auspicious work; cruel acts only** — input: karana.
> "In Vishti or Bhadra Karana, auspicious deeds shall not be done, but acts aimed at the ruin of enemies and those connected with poison may be done."
— same source L11959-11961. Corroborated: Raman L148 "Bhadra is unfit for any good work but is eminently suitable for violent and cruel deeds"; *Kalaprakasika* L2598 "Sakunam and Vishti Karanas should be avoided."

**R7. Fixed (sthira) karanas: specific uses** — input: karana.
> "In Sakuni Karana… increase of health and comfort, take medicine, learn/repeat Moola Mantras. In Chatushpada Karana… deeds connected with cows, Brahmins, Pitris and king. In Naga Karana… fixed-nature, cruel, stealth works. In Kimstughna Karana… any work for health and comfort as well as auspicious deeds."
— *Chidambaram BS* L11962-11967. (So Sakuni/Kimstughna are usable for medicine/general work — nuance beyond blanket avoidance.)

**R8. Bava good for starting permanent works; Taitila for marriage; Sakuni for kshudra-mantra initiation** — inputs: karana, activity.
> "Bava is auspicious for starting works of permanent importance while Thaithula is propitious for marriage… For getting initiation into kshudra mantras Sakuni Havana is propitious."
— *Jyotish_Muhurta_B.V. Raman.txt* L148.

**R9. Karana lords (for optional deity-weighting)** — input: karana.
> "Lords of 7 Karanas – Bava, Balava, Kaulava, Taitila, Gara, Vanija and Vishti are Indra, Brahma, Mitra, Aryama, Bhu, Sree and Yama." Fixed karanas "sacred to Rali(Raka), Vrisha, Sarpa and Vayu."
— *Chidambaram BS* L11938-11944.

## B2. Yoga (nityayoga) grading beyond Vyatipata/Vaidhriti

**R10. Nine+ bad yogas list** — input: yoga index.
> "The following nine Yogas are 'bad' – Vyaghatham, Parigham, Vajram, Vyatheepatham, Vydhrithi, Gandam, Athigandam, Sulam, Vishkambam."
— *Jyotish_1982_Kalaprakasika.txt* L8513-8515. Extended list (Raman): "Vyatipata, Dhruva, Mrityu, Ganda, Vajra, Soola, Vishkambha, Atiganda, Vyaghata and Parigha" (*Raman Muhurta* L520); core five: Atiganda(6), Soola(9), Ganda(10), Vyatipata(17), Vaidhriti(27) (Raman L148).

**R11. Good-yoga day marker** — input: yoga index.
> "A day marked by Subha, Amirtha, Siddha, Sri, Sobhana or Sutha Yoga asterism is good for starting all functions… tends to produce lasting benefits." Also "all tithis except Rikthai coinciding with the aforesaid [fortunate] Yogas produce good."
— *Kalaprakasika* L10129-10132, L10175-10177. (Subha=23, Amrita=5, Siddha=21, Sobhana=24 among nityayogas.)

**R12. Midday decay of yoga doshas** — input: yoga + local time vs noon.
> "The adverse qualities of Vishti, Vyatheepatham and Vaidhrithi, Janma-Nakshathra and the 5th asterism therefrom do not prevail after midday." Cf. Raman: "the aspects attributed to Vyatipatam, Vaidruti etc. become defunct after midday."
— *Kalaprakasika* L9784-9787; *Raman Muhurta* L181.

## C. Weekday–tithi–nakshatra triple combinations (Siddha / Vinasa / Visha Yogas)

**R13. Per-weekday Siddha Yoga tables (grade UP)** — inputs: vara + tithi + nakshatra. All fully computable lookup tables:
> "Sunday coinciding with Prathamai, Chathurthi, Shashti, Sapthami or Dhwadhasi and ruled by asterisms Pushya, Hastha, Uthara-palguni-ashada, Mula, Sravana or Utharabadhrapadha produces Siddha Yoga."
— *Kalaprakasika* L10179-10185; full Monday–Saturday tables at L10192-10269.
Cross-source (different table tradition, Raman):
> "Sunday coinciding with the 1st, 4th, 6th, 7th or 12th lunar day and ruled by Pushya, Hasta, Uttara, Uttarashadha, Moola, Sravana or Uttarabhadra gives rise to Siddha Yoga… Thursday identical with the 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th, 13th or 14th lunar day and Makha, Pushya, Punarvasu, Swati, Poorvashadha, Poorvabhadra, Revati or Aswini…"
— *Raman Muhurta* L192-196. NOTE: the two traditions disagree on details — implement one table per source, cite which.

**R14. Class-based Siddha yogas (vara + tithi-class)** — inputs: vara + tithi class only (cheap rule!).
> "A Friday coinciding with Nanda (1st, 6th, 11th), Wednesday identical with Bhadra (2nd, 7th, 12th), Tuesday coinciding with Jaya (3rd, 8th, 13th), Saturday falling on a Riktha tithi (4th, 9th, 14th), and Thursday falling on 5th, 10th or 15th (Poorna) constitute Siddha Yoga."
— *Raman Muhurta* L199; parallel in *Kalaprakasika* L10209 (Tuesday+Nanda/Jaya), L10221 (Wednesday+Bhadra/Jaya), L10245 (Friday+Nanda/Bhadra), L10259 (Saturday+Bhadra/Rikta).

**R15. Amita (Amrita) Siddha Yoga (vara + nakshatra only)** — inputs: vara + nakshatra.
> "Sunday to Saturday respectively coinciding with the constellations Hasta, Sravana, Aswini, Anuradha, Pushya, Revati and Rohini will give rise to Amita Siddha Yoga."
— *Raman Muhurta* L200.

**R16. Vinasa (fatal) & Visha yogas (grade DOWN)** — same inputs as R13.
> "Nasa yoga (fatal) — Sunday coinciding with Thrithiyai, Chathurthi, Ashtami, Navami, Thrayodhasi and Chathurdasi and with asterisms Bharani, Mrigasirsha, Aslesha, Visakha, Anuradha, Jyeshta or Sravishta… Visha yoga — Sunday coinciding with Panchami and asterism Krithika."
— *Kalaprakasika* L10186-10191; Vinasa tables for each weekday L10204-10269.

**R17. Unfavourable weekday-nakshatra pairs** — inputs: vara + nakshatra.
> "General – Sunday ruled by Aswini, Monday by Chithra, Tuesday by Utharashada, Wednesday by Sravishta, Thursday by Utharapalguni, Friday by Jyeshta and Saturday by Revathi are all unfavourable days."
— *Kalaprakasika* L10270-10275.

**R18. Powerful evil-quelling weekday-nakshatra pairs (grade UP, strong)** — inputs: vara + nakshatra.
> "Thursday ruled by Aswini, Punarvasu, Pushya, Magha or Swathi makes a Yoga before which all malefic effects flee as cotton before the wind…" (full Sun–Saturn lists L10144-10173.)
— *Kalaprakasika* L10143-10173.

## D. Moon-in-sign (gochara) rules for undertaking work

**R19. Chandranukulya — favourable Moon positions from janma rasi** — inputs: current moon sign vs natal moon sign.
> "…when the Moon is auspicious i.e. passing through the 1st, 3rd, 6th, 7th, 10th or 11th with respect to the Janma-rasi."
— *Jyotish_Brihat Samhita_M.R.Bhat_part 2_1981.txt* L21270-21272 (BS XCVIII shave chapter; classical general gochara-good set). Complements existing Chandrashtama (8th) penalty.

**R20. Moon transiting benefic-point-less AV signs → avoid auspicious functions** (optional, needs ashtakavarga data)
> "(b) When the Moon transits a sign containing no benefic points in her AV, auspicious functions should be avoided in such Nakshatras. (c) Note the Nakshatra occupied by the lord of the 8th from the Moon sign; when the Moon transits [that and] 3 Nakshatras therefrom, troubles, diseases and grief will come to pass."
— *Jyotish_Sambhu Hora Prakasa_R. Santhanam.txt* L6516-6525 (ch. 9 §81-87).

## E. Hora (planetary hour) quality rules

**R21. Hora lord sequence** — input: weekday + hour index.
> "The rst Hora [of the day belongs to the day lord]; the 3rd, by the planet sixth in order from the lord of the 2nd Hora. Thus the lord of each Hora [is] … the lord of the previous Hora, the lord of the 1st Hora being [the day-lord]."
— *Kalaprakasika* L8907-8917 (standard Chaldean-discard order; computable from vara alone).

**R22. Hora-lord outcome semantics (activity-matched hora)** — inputs: hora lord + intended activity.
> "The Influence of the Hora Lords – The Sun portends danger to life; Venus favours marriage; Mercury foretells child-birth; the Moon gives well-being; Saturn signifies fetters; Jupiter bestows pecuniary gains; Mars shows battle."
— *Kalaprakasika* L9109-9113. Implementable as per-hora sign: Jupiter/Moon/Venus horas positive, Saturn/Mars/Sun horas negative or activity-specific (marriage→Venus hora good; litigation/conflict→Mars hora).

**R23. Day-lord strength cancels weekday blemish** — needs chart, optional
> "No day of the week is 'bad', provided the lord thereof is strong in the figure of the heavens." / Raman: "No day of the week is blemished if the lord thereof is strongly placed in the election chart."
— *Kalaprakasika* L9791-9794; *Raman Muhurta* L181.

**R24. Weekday-time-of-day interaction** — inputs: vara + time.
> "Of Saturday, Sunday and Tuesday the day-times are 'bad'… Tuesday is not evil after mid-day. The adverse effects of Sunday, Tuesday and Saturday do not prevail after nightfall."
— *Kalaprakasika* L8506-8508, L9784, L9791-9792.

## F. Other computable doshas / neutralizations

**R25. Dhina-Mrityu & Dhina-Roga nakshatra-pada windows (daytime-only evil)** — inputs: nakshatra + pada + day/night.
> "Dhina-Mrityu – the first quarter of Sravishta, Hastha; the second quarter of Visakha and Ardhra; the third quarter of Utharabadhrapada and Aslesha; the fourth quarter of Bharani and Mula… powerful only if it occurs during day-time." Dhina-Roga: 1st qtr Aslesha/U.Bhadrapada; 2nd Bharani/Mula; 3rd U.Phalguni/Sravana; 4th Swati/Mrigasirsha — "devoid of evil if they occur during night."
— *Kalaprakasika* L8520-8535.

**R26. Aseevisham (ashubha nakshatra count from amavasya end)** — inputs: nakshatra + tithi boundary.
> "The 2nd, 6th, 9th, 11th, 13th, 18th and 26th asterisms from the one in which New-Moon ends are Aseevisham. This Yoga is inauspicious beyond remedy."
— *Kalaprakasika* L8536-8539.

**R27. Ekargala** — inputs: yoga + Sun/Moon nakshatra positions; daytime-only.
> "This evil Yoga, powerful only during the day-time, interferes in affairs started under the nine Yogas, Vishkambham, Vyatheepatham, Sooiam, Parigham, Gandam, Athigandam, Vajram, Vyaghatham, Vaidhrithi which are respectively governed by the nine asterisms Aswini, Aslesha, Mrigasirsha, Magha, Mula, Anuradha, Pushya, Punarvasu and Chithra" — formed when the day's Sun-nakshatra and Moon-nakshatra fall on the same line of the 3×3 diagram built from the governing asterism.
— *Kalaprakasika* L8540-8570.

**R28. Benefic neutralization clauses (cancellations to implement alongside penalties)** — needs election chart (optional layer):
> "Benefics placed in quadrants dispel the evil effects of Rikthai and Vishti; if the trines be powerful they possess the same influence… Jupiter or Venus well-dignified in a quadrant radically frustrates the evil effects of Vishti… Jupiter or Venus occupying the rising sign or aspecting it counteracts the effects of an inauspicious Thithi, day, asterism or rising sign."
— *Kalaprakasika* L9795-9813.

**R29. Tithi-Gandanta windows (already have nakshatra Gandanta in CONSISTENCY — this is the tithi variant)** — inputs: tithi + elapsed fraction.
> "The last 2 ghatis (48 minutes) of the 5th, 10th and 15th (Full Moon) and the first 2 ghatis of the 6th, 11th and 1st (dark half) lunar days go under tithigandanthara and they should be rejected for all new works."
— *Raman Muhurta* L161.

**R30. Shave-specific composite rule (worked example of multi-factor day grade)** — inputs: tithi, vara, karana, moon-from-janma-rasi.
> "It is not advisable to have a shave … on Saturdays, Tuesdays and Sundays, in Rikta lunar days (4th, 9th, 14th), on a day that is 9th from that of the previous shave, and in the Visti Karana." (Chidambaram adds: "when Karana is Bhadra".)
— *Bhat BS part 2* L21256-21261; *Chidambaram BS* L11908-11909.
